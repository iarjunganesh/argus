Know Your Customer — or KYC — is one of the most expensive and time-consuming tasks in financial compliance.

Before a bank can onboard a new customer, it must verify identity, check sanctions lists, screen for adverse media, resolve corporate ownership, and assess regulatory risk.

Doing this manually takes days. And the cost of getting it wrong can be enormous.

This is ARGUS.

ARGUS — Agentic Risk and Governance Unified Screening — is a multi-agent KYC system built on Azure AI Foundry, created for the Microsoft Agents League Hackathon 2026.

A single KYC request is broken into four specialist agents running in parallel — Identity, Screening, Corporate Intelligence, and Transaction Intelligence. Their results are then passed to the Compliance and Risk Agent as a fan-in step, all coordinated by an Orchestrator using the Agent-to-Agent protocol.

The knowledge retrieval layer is powered by Foundry IQ, with three knowledge bases — one for regulatory text, one for sanctions data, and one for adverse media. Every answer is cited, grounded, and traceable. No hallucination is possible.

All data in this demonstration is fully synthetic.

Let me show you ARGUS in action.

This is the ARGUS demo interface, built with Gradio and FastAPI.

I am submitting a KYC request for a synthetic corporate entity called Cayman Synth Capital — registered in the Cayman Islands, a high-risk jurisdiction under FATF guidelines.

I enter the entity name, select corporate, and set the jurisdiction to KY.

I click Submit.

Four agents fire in parallel immediately — Identity, Screening, Corporate Intelligence, and Transaction. The Orchestrator coordinates all four simultaneously.

Within seconds, all results return. The Orchestrator passes everything to the Compliance and Risk Agent — the fan-in step — which synthesises the final risk score.

The report is ready.

At the top of the report, ARGUS shows the overall risk tier, the numeric score, and the confidence for this run.

In the Regulatory Triggers section, every regulatory reference includes a Foundry IQ citation — the knowledge base name, the source document, and the article number.
