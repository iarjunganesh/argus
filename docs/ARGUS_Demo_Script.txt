Know Your Customer — or KYC — is one of the most expensive and time-consuming tasks in financial compliance.

Before a bank can onboard any new customer, it must verify their identity, check global sanctions lists, screen for adverse media, resolve corporate ownership structures, and assess regulatory risk.

Doing this manually takes days. And the cost of getting it wrong — through fines, fraud, or missed risks — can be enormous.

This is ARGUS.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
[SECTION 2 — 0:30 to 1:30 — WHAT IS ARGUS]
Screen: Keep architecture diagram visible
─────────────────────────────────────────────

ARGUS stands for Agentic Risk and Governance Unified Screening.

It is a multi-agent KYC risk assessment system built on Azure AI Foundry, created for the Microsoft Agents League Hackathon 2026, Reasoning Agents track.

A single KYC request is broken down into four specialist agents running in parallel, followed by a compliance fan-in step.

The Identity Agent verifies who the entity is. The Screening Agent checks global sanctions lists and adverse media. The Corporate Intelligence Agent resolves the ultimate beneficial ownership structure. The Transaction Intelligence Agent detects suspicious financial patterns. And the Compliance and Risk Agent synthesises everything into a final risk decision after those four upstream agents complete.

These agents communicate using the Agent-to-Agent protocol — called A2A — coordinated by a central Orchestrator on Azure AI Foundry.

The knowledge retrieval layer is powered by Foundry IQ — Microsoft's managed intelligence layer — with three knowledge bases. One for regulatory text, one for sanctions data, and one for adverse media. The adverse-media corpus now mixes synthetic cases with public-source summaries so the demo can show both controlled and real-world-style examples. Every answer is cited, grounded, and traceable.

Customer and transaction records in this demonstration are synthetic. Public-source adverse-media summaries are included to demonstrate realistic screening and citation behavior.

This matters deeply in compliance. A risk decision that cannot be traced to a specific regulatory source has no legal standing. ARGUS solves this by design, using Foundry IQ as the intelligence layer.
